Sources of the tafsir and word-meaning files in this folder
==========================================================

The texts are copied unaltered (no wording is added, removed or changed); the build scripts only regroup them by
ayah range (and tidy spacing and line breaks). All rights in the texts remain with their authors and
publishers; they are shown with attribution, free of charge, for non-commercial religious education.

Dataset (except تفسير السعدي): spa5k/tafsir_api, tag v1.2.3 (MIT licence for the dataset/code, https://github.com/spa5k/tafsir_api),
an export of the Quranic Universal Library (QUL) by Tarteel (https://qul.tarteel.ai/resources/tafsir).

tafsir_muyassar.json.gz   التفسير الميسر — نخبة من العلماء، مجمع الملك فهد لطباعة المصحف الشريف
                          QUL resource 38 (ar-tafsir-muyassar). Built by tools/build_tafsir.py.

tafsir_saadi.json.gz      تيسير الكريم الرحمن في تفسير كلام المنان (تفسير السعدي) — الشيخ عبد الرحمن بن ناصر السعدي
                          (ت ١٣٧٦هـ / 1956)، دار ابن الجوزي — as published by islamweb.net (المكتبة الإسلامية،
                          الكتاب 209): https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/209/<page>, pages 1–1747; each section
                          records its page. Built by tools/build_tafsir_saadi.py (polite sequential download).
                          The page text is kept as is; only islamweb's links, tooltips and «[ص: n]» page numbers are
                          removed, spacing is tidied, and quoted Quran (shown in colour on islamweb) is marked ﴿…﴾.
                          Sections follow the book's own «(n)» / «(n - m)» markers and passages; where islamweb
                          splits one ayah over two pages, those parts are joined into one section (17 cases).
                          6:105–107: islamweb lists these ayat (page 425) with no commentary, so they have no
                          Saadi section (nothing from another edition is substituted). Left out as not ayah
                          commentary: the volume prefaces (pages 205, 664, 850, 1121, 1309, 1490) and three short
                          lead-ins before a page's header ayat (pages 486, 509, 1676) and a surah title (1374).
                          Checked: 30 sampled ayat match islamweb's per-ayah tafsir pages word for word (one
                          differs only where islamweb glues a word to a quoted ayah, «أنهلم يلد»).

tafsir_mukhtasar.json.gz  المختصر في تفسير القرآن الكريم — إصدار مركز تفسير للدراسات القرآنية (الرياض)
                          QUL resource 251 (ar-tafsir-al-mukhtasar). Built by tools/build_tafsirs.py.
                          Checked against QuranEnc's «arabic_mokhtasar» edition: identical for all 6236 ayat.

gharib_muyassar.json.gz   الميسر في غريب القرآن الكريم — مجمع الملك فهد لطباعة المصحف الشريف
                          QUL resource 519 (al-muyassar-fi-al-gharib). Built by tools/build_tafsirs.py.
                          Shown in the ayah sheet as «معاني الكلمات».
                          Two circulating copies differ in places (e.g. 2:6) and the Complex's site
                          (qurancomplex.gov.sa) could not be reached to settle it, so only the entries on which QUL's
                          copy and the KFGQPC copy in nresan/warattil (android/app/src/main/assets/ghareeb.json,
                          SHA-256 c5b02f70…eb1cb) agree, ignoring tashkeel and punctuation, are included.
